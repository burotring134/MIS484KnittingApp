Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 May 2026

About this policy

Threadia is a mobile application that converts photos into cross-stitch patterns. This policy explains what personal data Threadia collects, why we collect it, where it is stored, and the choices you have. We try to keep it short and specific — if anything is unclear, email us at support@threadia.app.

Threadia is operated by Burak Dindar, an individual developer based in Turkey. We are the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

1. Sign in with Apple

To create an account you sign in with Apple. Apple sends Threadia:

  • A stable Apple user identifier (Apple's sub claim) — this never identifies you to anyone outside Threadia and Apple.
  • Your email address, or Apple's private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email."
  • Your name, only on the very first sign-in and only if you choose to share it.

We use these to create and reauthenticate your account. We never share your Apple identifier or email with advertisers or other apps.

2. Photos you select

When you create a new pattern, the photo you pick is uploaded to our backend so it can be analysed and turned into a stitch chart. The raw photo is held in memory during processing only — it is not written to disk, not stored in a database, and not retained after the request completes. The resulting cross-stitch grid (a list of DMC color codes per cell) is stored against your account so you can reopen the project later.

3. Project data

Your projects (name, grid, color palette, which cells you have marked completed, timestamps) are stored under your account so the app can sync across devices. None of this contains personal information beyond the project name you choose.

4. Diagnostic logs

Our backend writes minimal request logs (timestamp, endpoint, HTTP status, anonymised IP). We use these to debug crashes and detect abuse. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs inside the mobile app. There is no Apple App Tracking Transparency prompt because Threadia does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use your data

  • Authenticate you and keep your session active (Apple identifier + a JWT we mint server-side).
  • Generate a stitch chart from the photo you submit and return it to your device (the photo itself is discarded immediately after).
  • Persist your projects so they survive a reinstall or appear on your second device.
  • Operate, debug, and secure the service.

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not use your data for advertising.

Third-party processors

To produce a stitch chart we currently use the following providers:

fal.ai
Style-pass on your uploaded image before it is quantised into DMC colors. The image is sent over an authenticated API call and is not retained by fal.ai for training, per their API terms.
MongoDB Atlas / self-hosted MongoDB
Stores your account record and projects.
Apple App Store Connect
Distributes the app and provides Sign in with Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to data Apple holds.

We do not currently share data with anyone else. If that changes we will update this page and the effective date above.

Where data lives

Threadia's backend runs on servers located in the European Union. Your project data and account record are stored there. Apple Sign-In tokens are verified against Apple's public keys and are not stored. We use HTTPS for every connection between the app and the backend.

How long we keep it

  • Account record (Apple identifier, email, name): until you ask us to delete it.
  • Projects: until you delete them in-app, or until your account is deleted.
  • Uploaded photos: not retained — discarded as soon as the chart is generated.
  • Request logs: rotated within 30 days.

Your rights

Under GDPR and similar privacy laws you can ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal data we hold about you.
  • Export a copy of your projects and account record.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your account and every project linked to it.
  • Withdraw consent to Sign in with Apple — see the Manage your Apple ID page for revoking app access.

Email support@threadia.app to make any of these requests. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Children

Threadia is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has signed up, email us and we will delete the account.

Security

All traffic between the app and the backend is encrypted over HTTPS. Access tokens are short-lived JWTs signed with a secret stored only on the backend. Apple Distribution credentials and App Store Connect API keys are stored on Expo's servers, never in the app bundle. We follow standard practices to keep our servers patched and access-controlled, but no system is impenetrable — if you discover a security issue, please report it to support@threadia.app so we can fix it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the effective date at the top of the page and, for significant changes, surface a notice in the app on next launch. Older versions are available on request.
